What changed, and why

Changes made on October 6, after this reporting week
Budget moved from campaigns with no purchases to the two that are held back by budget
Hardware Search, Gas Bottle and Composite Decking spent €275.91 in the week with no purchases. At the same time, Home Heating Oil Search and Shopping – Firewood were each losing about a third of their possible impressions to budget while converting well. So: Hardware Search €25 to €10 a day (the figure you gave for the saw), Composite Decking paused, Home Heating Oil Search €80 to €90, Shopping – Firewood €15 to €25. The goal is more real profit from the same total spend, not more spend. I will review it after 7 days against your actual orders.
Gas Bottle & Cylinders: fixes to a campaign that was not converting
One search term was taking most of the spend (€190 of €291 in 30 days) without a purchase, so I tightened it. I added negative keywords for information-only searches, and removed some older exclusions that were blocking relevant searches (including the Flo Gas brand you sell). The ads now go to the propane cylinders page, which lists the 47kg and 34kg bottles that sell best. I will review it on 20 October, and if it still produces no purchases I will pause it and move its €10/day to Shopping – Firewood.
What stood out in the week
DEWALT FlexVolt Cut Off Saw: no sale yet
No order for the saw appears in WooCommerce through 5 October. The Google Search ad group has had very little traffic (a handful of clicks and about €1 of spend through 1 October), because the saw is not in the Shopping feed and its five keywords barely serve. Hardware is now at €10/day, in line with your instruction; I will look again on Friday 9 October.
Shopping – Firewood: first full read is positive
In its first two weeks (21 Sep to 5 Oct) the campaign made 15 conversions on €226.69 of spend, about €15 each, at an average click cost of €0.32. Google's own value for these is about 11 times the spend, which I will check against your real firewood orders before treating it as profit.

Methodology & caveats

  • Real revenue basis: WooCommerce "Net Sales" column (excluding VAT and shipping), 212 valid orders Sep 28–Oct 4 and 137 valid orders Sep 21–27 (refunded orders excluded from both).
  • Comparison basis: Monday to Sunday weeks. Sep 28–Oct 4 is compared with the week before it, Sep 21–27, each built from the same WooCommerce export and live Google Ads data for its own exact dates.
  • Channel attribution: orders tagged exactly "Source: Google" are counted as Google-driven. "Organic: Google" (unpaid search: 48 orders, €17,064.79 this week) is never added to this total.
  • Standing exclusion: Compost and Sand and Stones revenue tagged "Source: Google" is excluded, since no Google Ads campaign targets those categories.
  • Category resolution: a share of orders carry no product details in the export, almost entirely heating oil, and are resolved to Heating oil on the same verified method as every earlier report. Named products that fit no category (bulk deals, lubricants, coolant, pumps) are shown as revenue only.
  • Gross profit: category margins supplied by you (heating oil 10%, firewood 25%, solid fuel 25%, gas 20%, hardware 15%). Products with no supplied margin are excluded from profit rather than given a guessed margin.
  • Kenergy Solar: excluded from all real-profit math and every real-vs-platform ratio.
  • Live platform data: pulled from Google Ads for the exact windows on October 6, 2026.
