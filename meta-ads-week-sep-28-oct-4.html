Methodology & caveats

  • Real orders basis: WooCommerce "Net Sales" column (excluding VAT and shipping), valid orders only (refunded orders excluded), for Sep 28–Oct 4 and Sep 21–27.
  • Meta attribution rule: an order counts as Meta-driven if its attribution carries the campaign tag, a Paid+Social tag, or a Facebook referral. Generic Instagram or Facebook source tags without a campaign are not counted, so verified figures are a floor.
  • Gross profit: category margins supplied by you. The verified orders in the previous week were all in the firewood range (25%).
  • Consent and tracking limit: visitors who decline marketing cookies cannot be tracked for ad attribution by any method, so the gap between Meta's claim and your orders is partly that and partly Meta counting views that never led to a click.
  • Live platform data: pulled from Meta for the exact windows on October 6, 2026.
