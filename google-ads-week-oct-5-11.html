Methodology & caveats

  • Real revenue basis: WooCommerce "Net Sales" column (excluding VAT and shipping), 275 valid orders Oct 5–11 from a fresh export downloaded October 12 (refunded orders excluded). The Sep 28–Oct 4 figures (212 valid orders) are those published in last week's report.
  • Cross-check: the order export was checked against WooCommerce’s own Products report for the same dates: €115,479.00 and 329 items in the orders export against €115,478.99 and 329 items in the Products report. Every Google-tagged order outside heating oil was matched to a named product; the 68 Google-tagged orders (€37,018.30) with no product listed in the export are counted as heating oil, so profit on them is at the lowest margin (10%) and cannot be overstated.
  • Tagging caveat: the "Source: Google" tag comes from WooCommerce's own order attribution. It can include some visits that were not ad clicks and miss others (visitors who decline cookies), so treat the verified figure as a close estimate of Google-driven sales, not an exact count.
  • Platform figures still maturing: Google keeps crediting conversions for several days after a week ends. The previous week's Google figures are restated to a fresh pull on October 12; this week's will most likely rise. Verified (WooCommerce) figures are not affected.
  • Comparison basis: Monday to Sunday weeks. Oct 5–11 is compared with the week before it, Sep 28–Oct 4, using live Google Ads data for each window's exact dates.
  • Channel attribution: orders tagged exactly "Source: Google" are counted as Google-driven. "Organic: Google" (unpaid search: 60 orders, €25,201.30 this week) is never added to this total.
  • Standing exclusion: Compost and Sand and Stones revenue tagged "Source: Google" is excluded, since no Google Ads campaign targets those categories.
  • Category resolution: a share of orders carry no product details in the export, almost entirely heating oil, and are resolved to Heating oil on the same verified method as every earlier report. Named products that fit no category (bulk deals, lubricants, coolant, pumps) are shown as revenue only.
  • Gross profit: category margins supplied by you (heating oil 10%, firewood 25%, solid fuel 25%, gas 20%, hardware 15%). Products with no supplied margin are excluded from profit rather than given a guessed margin.
  • Kenergy Solar: excluded from all real-profit math and every real-vs-platform ratio.
  • Live platform data: pulled from Google Ads for both windows on October 12, 2026.
