Methodology & caveats

  • Real revenue basis: WooCommerce "Net Sales" column (excluding VAT and shipping), 1090 valid orders for October and 891 for September, from fresh exports downloaded November 2 (refunded orders excluded; September is taken from the September 1 to October 31 export by date, and the October orders in that export are identical to the October-only export). Orders were checked against WooCommerce’s own Products report for the same dates: October agrees within 0.36% (€447,730.86 against €449,357.58). The September orders are 1.84% (€6,668.02) lower than the Products report, the same direction and size as in earlier reports; I could not match it to specific orders. If all of it were Google-driven firewood at the 25% margin, September’s real net would be up to about €1,667.01 higher, which would still leave it below October’s. Heating oil agrees closely with WooCommerce’s own totals once every order with no product listed is treated as heating oil (October: 630 orders and €341,593.07 against 642 and €349,849.42; September: 427 and €238,875.98 against 426 and €241,813.25 across the two heating oil products).
  • Same period last year: October 2025 from wc-orders-report-export-17935740505918.csv, dates checked in code 2025-10-01 to 2025-10-31, 1077 rows, 1061 valid orders (€314,887.36), 16 refunded rows excluded (the 8 repeated order numbers are refund pairs, none among the valid orders). Against WooCommerce’s own Products report (€322,835.84) the orders file is 2.46% (€7,948.48) lower, the same direction and size as in the September 2026 file; the gap sits in heating oil (707 orders naming Kerosene or blank, €229,932.49, against 694 and €235,575.62 in the Products report), so heating oil is slightly understated in 2025 (2.5%), as it is in October 2026 (2.4%), and the year-on-year growth is not materially affected. 698 of the 1061 orders (66%) have a blank Product(s) field in 2025 and are treated as heating oil on the same method as 2026; the Products report supports that at report level (it counts 694 Kerosene orders for October 2025), but it was not checked order by order. Platform spend for 2025 was pulled from Google Ads and Meta on November 2, 2026.
  • September restated: September here is recalculated from a fresh export with the current category rules, so it differs slightly from the September monthly report (real net +€12,110.96 here against +€12,401.21 there, on 891 orders and €355,685.18 here against 892 and €355,761.60). The Google-tagged total (371 orders, €150,946.31) is identical.
  • Comparison basis: October 1–31 is compared with September 1–30, using live Google Ads data for each month’s exact dates. The weekly figures above split the month at Monday to Sunday weeks, with the first and last weeks shortened to the month’s edges.
  • Channel attribution: orders tagged exactly "Source: Google" are counted as Google-driven. "Organic: Google" (unpaid search: 253 orders, €99,946.45 in October) is never added to this total.
  • Tagging caveat: paid Google clicks have carried tracking tags since the fix on 8 July, so "Source: Google" now mostly means a paid click. The tag comes from WooCommerce’s own order attribution. It can include some visits that were not ad clicks and miss others (visitors who decline cookies), so treat the verified figure as a close estimate of Google-driven sales, not an exact count.
  • Standing exclusion: Compost and Sand and Stones revenue tagged "Source: Google" is excluded (€4,029.40 in October), since no Google Ads campaign targets those categories. Widening that rule would add at most about €1,007.35 of profit and would not change any budget decision.
  • Category resolution: every order was categorised once, before any date split.
