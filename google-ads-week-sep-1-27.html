Methodology & caveats

  • Real revenue basis: WooCommerce "Net Sales" column (ex-VAT, ex-shipping), 785 valid orders Sep 1–27 and 452 valid orders Aug 1–27 (refunded orders excluded from both).
  • Comparison basis: Aug 1–27 vs Sep 1–27, a genuine 27-day-to-27-day month-over-month comparison, both built from fresh WooCommerce exports and live Google Ads API pulls for their own exact windows.
  • Channel attribution: orders tagged exactly "Source: Google" counted as Google-driven. "Organic: Google" (unpaid search: 178 orders / €65,139.62 this period) is never summed into this total, since organic traffic has nothing to do with Google Ads spend.
  • Standing exclusion: Compost and Sand+Stones revenue tagged "Source: Google" is excluded: no Google Ads campaign targets these categories. Solid fuel remains included (the Shopping | Solid Fuel campaign genuinely targets it).
  • Category resolution: a share of orders don't carry product details in the export, almost entirely heating oil (kerosene, priced per litre, one-off listings that churn quickly). These are resolved to Heating Oil based on the same fully-verified method used in every report this month.
  • Gross profit: category margins supplied by you (heating oil 10%, firewood 25%, solid fuel 25%, gas 20%, hardware 15%, compost/sand 25%). Products with no supplied margin (Bulk Deals, lubricants, tanks, AdBlue) are shown as revenue only and excluded from all profit math, rather than assigned a guessed margin.
  • Kenergy Solar: excluded from all real-profit math and every real-vs-platform ratio. Its €12,000-per-conversion ADD rule remains paused pending a real closed-deal confirmation.
  • Live platform data: pulled fresh via Google Ads' own reporting API for the exact Sep 1–27 and Aug 1–27 windows, Sep 29 2026.
