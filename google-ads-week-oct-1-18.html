The headline

ReadReal profit after ad spend was about level: +€7,825.75 for 1–18 October against +€7,628.50 for the same 18 days of September (+2.6%), on real Google-driven revenue up 16.0% (€95,898.52 to €111,257.40). Profit grew far more slowly than revenue for two reasons. Ad spend rose 23.8% (€4,213.46 to €5,216.65), and sales moved toward the lower-margin product: heating oil revenue grew 23.5% (€78,373.89 to €96,816.43) at a 10% margin, while firewood fell 11.0% (€10,745.53 to €9,559.73) at a 25% margin. Gross profit rose 10.1% (€11,841.96 to €13,042.40), but spend grew faster. Each Google-driven order cost €19.99 in ad spend, against €18.98. The €197.25 difference in net profit is smaller than the uncertainty in the September figure (see the methodology notes), so I would read the two periods as level, not as a gain.

One headline in each of the three Home Heating Oil ads contained a phone number, which Google does not allow in ad text and which left the ads only partly approved. I removed it, and all three are now fully approved.
October 8 and 12: delivery settings applied to product listings
Heating oil and kiln dried firewood crate listings were being held out of Shopping because they arrived without a delivery setting. I applied the setting to the current listings on both dates. Shopping – Heating Oil, which had spent almost nothing in September, spent €25.36 and recorded 2 conversions in October.

Methodology & caveats

  • Real revenue basis: WooCommerce "Net Sales" column (excluding VAT and shipping), 590 valid orders for Oct 1–18 and 617 for Sep 1–18, both from fresh exports downloaded October 19 (refunded orders excluded). Orders were checked against WooCommerce's own Products report for the same dates: October agrees within 0.13% (€245,931.19 against €245,603.78). The September export is 2.2% (€5,539.64) lower than the Products report. Heating oil now agrees within 0.6% (€176,078.22 against €177,094.71), once every order with no product listed is treated as heating oil, which WooCommerce’s own kerosene and variations reports confirm for all 291 of them. The remaining €4,523 (1.8%) sits in other products, mostly one firewood product (2.3M kiln dried oak, 18 items, €8,131.78), and I could not match it to specific orders. If all of it were Google-driven firewood at the 25% margin, September’s real net would be up to about €1,131 higher, so the October-versus-September profit difference is inside that range.
  • Year on year: a comparison with October 2025 is not included in this report.
  • Comparison basis: Oct 1–18 is compared with the same 18 days of September, using live Google Ads data for each window's exact dates. The two periods are not Monday-to-Sunday weeks, so this report does not replace the weekly numbers.
  • Channel attribution: orders tagged exactly "Source: Google" are counted as Google-driven. "Organic: Google" (unpaid search: 142 orders, €58,239.01 in October) is never added to this total.
  • Tagging caveat: paid Google clicks have carried tracking tags since the fix on 8 July, so "Source: Google" now mostly means a paid click. The tag comes from WooCommerce's own order attribution. It can include some visits that were not ad clicks and miss others (visitors who decline cookies), so treat the verified figure as a close estimate of Google-driven sales, not an exact count.
  • Standing exclusion: Compost and Sand and Stones revenue tagged "Source: Google" is excluded, since no Google Ads campaign targets those categories.
  • Category resolution: a share of orders carry no product details in the export, almost entirely heating oil, and are resolved to Heating oil on the same verified method as every earlier report, so profit on them is at the lowest margin (10%) and cannot be overstated. Named products that fit no category (bulk deals, lubricants, coolant, pumps) are shown as revenue only.
  • Gross profit: category margins supplied by you (heating oil 10%, firewood 25%, solid fuel 25%, gas 20%, hardware 15%).
  • Platform figures still maturing: Google keeps crediting conversions for several days after a period ends, so October's Google-reported figures will most likely rise. Verified (WooCommerce) figures are not affected.
  • Kenergy Solar: excluded from all real-profit math and every real-vs-platform ratio.
  • Live platform data: pulled from Google Ads for both windows on October 19, 2026.
