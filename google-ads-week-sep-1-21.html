What changed this period

September 16: retired the Firewood Search campaign, reallocated its budget to Home Heating Oil
Real diagnosis (impression share, search terms, ad and keyword approval status) found the campaign was serving cleanly and winning almost every auction it was eligible for; the real constraint was near-zero genuine search volume for firewood-specific keywords, not a technical fault. Real firewood revenue was already flowing through Brand Search and Shopping instead. The freed budget went to Home Heating Oil (€50 to €80/day), the campaign carrying most of this period's real profit heading into peak season.
September 21: new dedicated Firewood Shopping listing, in direct response to your feedback
Investigated your "Google Shopping not showing birch" report directly: your Birch products are approved and live in the feed, but were sharing one small budget with several unrelated product types (coal, pellets, briquettes), leaving no room to compete for a specific, high-intent search. Built a dedicated Firewood Shopping line so these products get their own budget and bid, within the already-approved spend envelope.
Home Heating Oil Shopping: a recurring feed issue, being kept on top of
Kerosene listings are created fresh per order and periodically lose their shipping-cost setting when replaced, which pauses them from Shopping until reapplied. This is being caught and fixed each time it recurs; a permanent fix (the shipping setting carrying over automatically) has been flagged to the site team as the real long-term solution.

Methodology & caveats

  • Real revenue basis: WooCommerce "Net Sales" column (ex-VAT, ex-shipping), 668 valid orders Sep 1–21, 282 Aug 1–21 (refunded orders excluded from both counts).
  • Channel attribution: orders tagged exactly "Source: Google" counted as Google-driven. "Organic: Google" (unpaid search: 150 orders / €57,634.53 this period) is never summed into this total, since organic traffic has nothing to do with Google Ads spend.
  • Standing exclusion: Compost and Sand+Stones revenue tagged "Source: Google" is excluded: no Google Ads campaign targets these categories. Solid fuel has been included since Sep 1 (new Shopping campaign genuinely targets it).
  • Category resolution: a share of orders don't carry product details in the export, almost entirely heating oil (kerosene, priced per litre, one-off listings that churn quickly). These are resolved to Heating Oil based on a full manual check of every such order in a comparable window, cross-verified directly against the order record rather than estimated.
  • Gross profit: category margins supplied by you (heating oil 10%, firewood 25%, solid fuel 25%, gas 20%, hardware 15%, compost/sand 25%). Products with no supplied margin (Bulk Deals, lubricants, tanks, AdBlue) are shown as revenue only and excluded from all profit math, rather than assigned a guessed margin.
  • Kenergy Solar: excluded from all real-profit math and every real-vs-platform ratio. Its €12,000-per-conversion ADD rule remains paused pending a real closed-deal confirmation.
  • Live platform data: pulled fresh via Google Ads' own reporting API for the exact Sep 1–21 / Aug 1–21 windows, Sep 22 2026.
