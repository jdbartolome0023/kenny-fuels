What changed this month

Shopping campaigns launched, then trimmed to what works
Shopping campaigns for Hardware, Solid Fuel, Gas, Fencing and Heating Oil went live in early September. After about ten days of data I paused Fencing (€93.97, no conversions) and Gas (€95.19, almost none) on Sep 10, because Gas Bottle & Cylinders Search already covers gas. On Sep 21 I launched a dedicated Shopping – Firewood campaign at €15 a day, after you flagged that birch kiln crates weren't appearing.
Conversion tracking, goals and bidding tidied across every Search campaign
On Sep 3 I reviewed all seven Search campaigns: conversion goals, Google's automated search expansion setting, and bidding. The cost per acquisition target on Home Heating Oil Search was removed so it could scale with demand.
Ad schedules and budgets set from real order data
Brand Search now runs Monday to Saturday, 8am to 9pm, with no Sunday. Heating Oil runs Monday to Saturday, 9am to 9pm. Hardware is narrowed to 9am to 8pm. Kenergy Solar is off on Fridays and Saturdays, where it had a repeated zero-conversion pattern. On Sep 8 I moved €20 a day of Brand Search budget (€170 to €150) to fund Heating Oil Shopping.
Location targeting fixed to people physically in your delivery counties
On Sep 15 I changed six campaigns from "presence or interest" to "presence", so ads only reach people actually in, not just interested in, Carlow, Wicklow, Wexford, Dublin and Waterford (Heating Oil stays at three counties because kerosene cannot be delivered to Dublin or Waterford).
Firewood Search retired, firewood moved into Shopping
The standalone Firewood Search campaign had almost no impressions after late August despite fixes. Real firewood orders kept rising through Brand Search and Shopping, so on Sep 16 I retired it and extended Shopping to cover firewood.
Sep 25: DEWALT FlexVolt Cut Off Saw test, at your request
Built inside the Hardware campaign (budget raised €15 to €25 a day). Through Oct 1 it has had 2 clicks, cost about €1 and made no sales. The saw isn't in the Shopping feed yet. I'll review it again on Friday 9 October.
Heating Oil Shopping feed kept running
Kerosene listings are created fresh per order and periodically lose their shipping setting, which stops them showing in Shopping until it is reapplied. I caught and fixed this each time it recurred this month. A permanent fix on the website side is still the real long-term answer.

Methodology & caveats

  • Real revenue basis: WooCommerce "Net Sales" column (excluding value added tax and shipping), 892 valid orders in September and 532 in August (refunded orders excluded: 18 and 4).
  • Comparison basis: full calendar months, Sep 1–30 against Aug 1–31, built from WooCommerce exports and live Google Ads pulls for their own exact windows.
  • Channel attribution: orders tagged exactly "Source: Google" are counted as Google-driven. "Organic: Google" (unpaid search: 202 orders, €71,903.43 in September) is never summed into this total.
  • Standing exclusion: Compost, Sand+Stones and building materials revenue tagged "Source: Google" is excluded, because no Google Ads campaign targets those categories. Solid fuel remains included (Shopping – Solid Fuel genuinely targets it).
  • Category resolution: a share of orders carry no product details in the export, almost entirely heating oil (kerosene, priced per litre, one-off listings). These are resolved to Heating Oil using the same method as every earlier report this month. Orders with several categories are assigned to one, in the order Heating oil, Firewood, Solid fuel, Gas, Hardware.
  • Gross profit: category margins supplied by you (heating oil 10%, firewood 25%, solid fuel 25%, gas 20%, hardware 15%). Products with no supplied margin are shown as revenue only, not guessed.
  • Google Analytics 4: property 396621665, pulled live for the exact Sep 1–30 and Aug 1–31 windows. Used for traffic, channel and landing page trends only; it records fewer purchases than WooCommerce because visitors who decline cookies are not counted.
  • Attribution tagging: since the Jul 8 tagging fix, paid Google clicks arrive as "Source: Google" and unpaid search as "Organic: Google"; both tag cleanly throughout the September export.
  • Kenergy Solar: excluded from all real-profit math and every real-against-platform ratio.
  • Year on year: only spend, clicks and impressions are compared with September 2025; conversions from that month are not credible purchase counts.
  • Live platform data: pulled fresh from Google Ads for the exact Sep 1–30 and Aug 1–31 windows on Oct 2, 2026.
