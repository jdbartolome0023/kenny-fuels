The headline

ReadHeating season is now clearly showing up in real numbers, not just platform reporting. Spend is down slightly period over period (-5.3%) while real Google-driven revenue is up nearly 5.7 times (€11,178 to €63,594), and real net profit swung from a loss of €1,111 to €1,510 in the first 13 days of August to a profit of €5,153 to €7,676 over the same span in September. Heating Oil and Brand Search are carrying this, and the gap between what Google's dashboard claims and what actually sold kept narrowing (3.87x overstated in August, down to 1.70x now).

Ad spend, answered directly

ReadSpend came down slightly again this period. €2,963.68 (ex-Solar) over the first 13 days of September, 5.3% below the same 13 days in August, even with four new Shopping campaigns now live for the full period (two of them, Fencing and Gas, were paused September 10 after producing no real return, see What Changed below). Combined with Meta, which has been deliberately dialled down to near-zero from September 4, total spend across both platforms remains well under the approved combined budget.

What changed this period

September 7: fixed a bug that had silently restricted three campaigns to Saturday-only serving
Traced to a script run August 20 that meant to add a Saturday bid adjustment to Firewood, Kenergy Solar, and Composite Decking, but because none of the three had any day-of-week schedule at all beforehand, adding a single Saturday entry accidentally told Google to serve only on Saturdays. Removed September 7. Kenergy Solar and Composite Decking recovered immediately; Firewood did not, and is a separate open issue (see below).
September 10: paused Shopping – Fencing and Shopping – Gas after zero real return
Both ran nine full days with real spend (€93–95 each) and produced no real orders of any kind. Gas Bottle & Cylinders already covers gas demand through Search. Budget freed here funded the new Heating Oil Shopping listing below, no net change to total spend.
September 10: new Heating Oil Shopping campaign went live
Kerosene product listings cleared their shipping-setup issue and are now approved in the feed. Heating oil is the account's biggest real category, so this is a natural extension, funded by the Fencing/Gas pause above rather than new budget.
September 9: attached a past-customer list to Brand Search in observation mode
A cleaned Mailchimp opt-in list is now linked as a reporting signal on Brand Search, not yet affecting bids. Once enough data builds up, this can show whether past customers convert differently on branded search, and inform a bid adjustment.
Open technical issue
  • OpenFirewood Search campaign still not serving properly, an issue that predates this period (since roughly August 23) and survived the September 7 schedule fix and a manual pause/reactivate. Every setting checked on the account side is correct; this now looks like something only Google can see. Real revenue impact has been minimal: firewood orders are up through the period regardless, picked up by Brand Search and the Shopping campaigns instead.
Budget position
  • SearchCore Search campaigns spent broadly flat versus the same period in August, with the mix shifting toward Brand Search and Home Heating Oil.
  • ShoppingNet €40/day across the four Shopping campaigns after the Sep 10 Fencing/Gas pause and Heating Oil addition (was €45/day at launch).
  • CombinedGoogle plus Meta running well under the approved daily budget, with Meta dialled to near-zero from Sep 4.

Methodology & caveats

  • Real revenue basis: WooCommerce "Net Sales" column (ex-VAT, ex-shipping), 396 valid orders Sep 1–13, 138 Aug 1–13 (refunded/cancelled/failed orders excluded from both counts).
  • Channel attribution: orders tagged exactly "Source: Google" counted as Google-driven. "Organic: Google" (unpaid search: 99 orders / €41,979.14 this period) is never summed into this total, under any framing, since organic traffic has nothing to do with Google Ads spend.
  • Standing exclusion: Compost and Sand+Stones revenue tagged "Source: Google" is excluded: no Google Ads campaign targets these categories. Solid fuel has been included since Sep 1 (new Shopping campaign); it carries no margin figure as none has been supplied.
  • Category fingerprinting: a meaningful share of orders don't carry product details in the export, overwhelmingly heating oil (kerosene, single line item, priced per litre). Orders resolved via a coupon-code check, an exact match to a known order's price, a repeated price among other unlabelled orders, then a price-range match against categories with enough known orders; anything left over is carried at a 10–25% margin range rather than assigned a category.
  • Gross profit: category margins supplied by you (heating oil 10%, firewood 25%, gas 20%, hardware 15%, compost/sand 25%). Solid fuel has no supplied margin and is excluded from all profit math.
  • Kenergy Solar: excluded from all real-profit math and every real-vs-platform ratio. Its €12,000-per-conversion ADD rule remains paused pending a real closed-deal confirmation.
  • Live platform data: pulled fresh via Google Ads' own reporting API (GAQL) for the exact Sep 1–13 / Aug 1–13 windows, Sep 15 2026.
